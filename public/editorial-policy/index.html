Editorial Policy

Topic selection

We prioritize clear reader questions, practical usefulness and topics that can be explained accurately.

Research and sourcing

We aim to prioritize primary sources, official documentation and authoritative references for claims that require verification. Time-sensitive details should be checked against current sources before publication.

AI-assisted writing

AI tools may support topic research, outlining, drafting, translation and editing. AI output is not automatically considered accurate or publishable.

Review process

Articles should be reviewed for correctness, clarity, originality, links, language quality and potential duplication. High-impact or rapidly changing subjects require extra care.

Images and copyright

We aim to use original or appropriately licensed images and provide attribution where the applicable licence requires it. Image licences should be checked individually before publication.

Corrections and updates

We review correction requests and update content when a meaningful correction is warranted. Substantial changes may be reflected in the update date.
