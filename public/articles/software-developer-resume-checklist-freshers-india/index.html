Software Developer Resume Checklist for Freshers in India

Make your first resume easier to scan with clear projects, honest skills, measurable details and links that recruiters can open.

A fresher resume should help a recruiter quickly answer three questions: what role are you targeting, what can you build, and what evidence supports your skills? You do not need to make every section large. You need to make the most relevant information easy to find and easy to verify.

Keep the first screen focused

For most entry-level software roles, a concise one-page resume is a practical starting point. Put your name and working contact details at the top, then add your education, relevant technical skills, strongest projects and experience or internships if you have them.

Use a readable font, consistent spacing and straightforward headings. Avoid decorative skill bars, complicated columns or icons that make essential details difficult to read when the resume is parsed by recruitment software.

Describe skills with evidence

List technologies that you can discuss and demonstrate. Group them by purpose—for example, programming languages, frameworks, databases and developer tools. Do not add a technology just because it appeared in a job description if you have never used it.

When a role asks for Flutter, JavaScript or backend skills, surface the relevant technologies near the top. Tailor the order to the role without claiming experience you do not have.

Turn projects into proof

Projects are often the strongest evidence on a fresher resume. For each project, state what it does, the main technologies, one meaningful engineering decision and the result. Be specific, but do not invent users, performance gains or business impact.

For example: ‘Built a Flutter weather app that retrieves forecast data from an API, handles loading and error states, and presents results in a responsive interface.’ This gives a reviewer more information than ‘Made a weather app’. Add a live demo or repository link when available and safe to share.

Make links and claims verifiable

Open your GitHub, portfolio and LinkedIn links in a private browser window before applying. Confirm that repositories are public where intended, README files explain how to run the project, and secrets such as API keys are not committed to source control.

Check dates, spelling, phone number and email address. If you include a CGPA or percentage, make sure it matches your official record and follows the format requested by the employer.

A final tailoring checklist

Before each application, compare your resume with the actual job description. Move relevant skills and projects forward, use clear action verbs and ensure the resume tells a consistent story. Keep a master resume with all truthful details, then make a focused version for each role.

Ask a peer or mentor to review the document and explain any line that sounds unclear. Prepare to discuss every project, trade-off and technology you include—the interview can go deeper than the resume itself.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a fresher resume be?

One page is a useful starting point for many entry-level roles. Include a second page only if you have relevant evidence that genuinely needs the space.

Should freshers include projects?

Yes, relevant personal, academic or open-source projects can demonstrate practical skills when described honestly and linked where possible.

Can I use AI to write my resume?

AI can help organize and edit your wording, but verify every detail and never include fabricated skills, experience or achievements.
